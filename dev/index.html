CompReS

CompReS is a Julia pseudospectral solver for forced-dissipated 2D turbulence. It covers the incompressible and the compressible (low Mach) model. One source runs on CPU threads, NVIDIA GPUs and AMD GPUs.

  • The binding equations are in docs/ns2d_equations.tex (specification).
  • The design decisions are in docs/adr/ (architecture decision records).
  • The work packages are in docs/ROADMAP.md.
