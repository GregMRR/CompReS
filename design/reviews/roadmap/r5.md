## Findings
### E45 [major] The compressible injection rates are tested only at `ρ = 1`, where every `ρ` factor is 1; a rate that drops `ρ` passes
- Where: ROADMAP:177 (WP-11 Accept: "with `ρ = 1`, compressible diagnostics, including the injection rates, equal the incompressible ones"); ROADMAP:176; ROADMAP:147 (the injection test is "an incompressible run"); ADR-0017:7-8.
- Evidence: `r5-E45.jl` (seed 5, N = 16, Δt = 1e-3). Reference: the exact forcing-only change `ΔE_K/Δt` and `ΔΩ/Δt` on the grid, with `ρ` held fixed.
  ```
  rho = 1                  E_K: |text - dE/dt| = 5.3e-13  |mutant - dE/dt| = 5.3e-13   Omega: |text - dO/dt| = 1.8e-12  |mutant - dO/dt| = 1.8e-12
  rho = 1 + 0.1 cos(x+y)   E_K: |text - dE/dt| = 2.1e-13  |mutant - dE/dt| = 3.3e-02   Omega: |text - dO/dt| = 2.8e-12  |mutant - dO/dt| = 5.2e-02
  ```
  The mutants are the incompressible formulas `⟨u·f⟩ + ½Δt⟨|f|²⟩` and `⟨ω curl f⟩ + ½Δt⟨(curl f)²⟩`. These are the formulas a WP-11 agent obtains by reusing the WP-8 method. At `ρ = 1` the mutants equal the correct rates. At `ρ ≠ 1` they are wrong by 6% (`E_K`) and 1.2% (`Omega`) of the rate. No other acceptance item covers the compressible rates.
- Consequence: a compressible `inj_E_kin` or `inj_Omega` without the `ρ` weights passes every blocking test. It reaches production `global.nc` with an error of order `Ma²` relative to the rate. This is the same order as the compressible effects the project measures, and it breaks the closure of the `E_K` and `Omega` budgets.
- Hint: add to WP-11 a forcing-only compressible test with nonuniform `ρ` (only `add_forcing!` in the right-hand side, so `ρ` stays fixed) that checks `inj = ΔE_K/Δt` and `ΔΩ/Δt` as the WP-7 test does.
