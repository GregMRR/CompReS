## Findings
### E1 [major] WP-11 tolerance test does not say whether `E_kin` and `Omega` are grid means or truncated spectral sums; the spectral reading fails the 10·eps bound
- Where: ROADMAP.md:179 (also :175, spec tex:166-178 defines `E_K = ½Σ|ŵ|²`, `Ω = ½Σ|ŝ|²`)
- Evidence: `python3 -I reviews/docs/ROADMAP/r7-probe-wtrunc.py`, N = 32, `ρ = 1 + 0.1 cos(x+y)`, random `u` in band 4..6. Relative gap between the truncated spectral sum and the grid mean: `E_kin` 7.5e-15 (34 eps), `Omega` 2.9e-13 (1300 eps). The grid mean equals the full (untruncated) spectral sum. `√ρ` and `1/ρ` are not band-limited, so truncating `ŵ`, `ŝ` drops weight. The injection formulas `⟨ρu·f⟩ + ½Δt⟨ρ|f|²⟩` and `⟨q curl f⟩ + ½Δt⟨|curl f|²/ρ⟩` are exact only against grid means.
- Consequence: an implementer who follows the spec (`Σ|ŝ|²` over retained modes) fails the blocking test in Float64 on a correct code, or weakens the tolerance.
- Hint: state that the GLOBAL `E_kin` and `Omega` are grid means of `ρ|u|²/2`, `ω²/(2ρ)` (or that the test uses these), and say how the spectral sums differ.
