## Findings
### E35 [major] WP-14's 1e-12 bound at N = 64 is below the round-off floor for Polytropic(γ = 1.4), and the floor grows with N (re-opens r2 E21)
- Where: ROADMAP:190; ADR-0015:8 ("the round-off test runs at N = 64").
- Evidence: `r3-E35.jl` follows the amended procedure exactly: spectral h, h₀ by bisection, ρ̂ truncated. It reports max|∂_tθ̂| / max|k_ik_j(u_iu_j)^| for the cellular flow at Ma = 0.1:
  - Isothermal: 1.0e-8 (N = 16), 2.8e-13 (32), 6.1e-13 (64), 4.3e-12 (128), 1.6e-11 (256).
  - Polytropic: 7.5e-10, 4.0e-13, **2.1e-12** (64), 5.7e-12, 1.5e-11.
  - Two other correct implementations give Polytropic N = 64 = 2.4e-12 (PATIENT plans) and 1.1e-12 (h − h(1) transformed). All three fail the bound.
- Mechanism: the polytropic h(1) = c_s²/(γ−1) is about 10³ times the variation of h. The pointwise round-off of h(ρ) then becomes spectral noise of size about eps·h(1)/N, and the factor k² up to k_max² amplifies it. The truncation residual falls with N, but this floor rises with N.
- Consequence: a correct WP-14 fails its blocking test. The agent then loosens the bound ad hoc or searches for a bug that does not exist. At larger N, even Isothermal fails.
- Hint: scale the bound with the floor, for example `C·eps·(h(1)/max|h − h(1)|)·(k_max/k_min)²`, or pass when the residual is at least 10⁴ below the uniform-ρ value at the same N.
- Resolution: _(author)_

### E36 [major] The sampling point of û relative to f̂ is still unfixed in the OU test, and the WP-8 diagnostic interface cannot see û "at the start of the step" (re-opens r2 E25)
- Where: ROADMAP:162 (WP-9 test: "the grid mean ⟨u·f⟩ … plus ½Δt⟨|f|²⟩", with no sampling point); ROADMAP:152 ("with û at the start of the step"); ROADMAP:144 (update before the first stage); ADR-0009:17 (a diagnostic is `(state, workspace) → value`).
- Evidence: `r3-probe-ou-test.jl` (seed 3, M = 4000, exactly the WP-9 set-up):
  - û at the start of the step: 1.0064 ± 0.0090, pass.
  - û after the step, same correct generator: **1.0164 ± 0.0090, FAIL**.
  - The bias is exactly +Δt/τ_f, because u_{n+1} = u_n + Δt f_n. The correction after the step would be −½Δt|f̂|², not +½Δt|f̂|².
  - Between steps (when the driver evaluates diagnostics), the state holds u_{n+1} while the forcing still holds f_n. So a `(state, workspace)` diagnostic computes the "after the step" case, unless `step!` stores û or the product.
- Consequence: the blocking OU test fails a correct forcing when it samples at the natural point. The `inj_` diagnostic in production carries a +Δt/τ_f bias, twice the bias that the term was added to remove.
- Hint: state "û and f̂ of the same step, û before `u += Δt f`" in WP-9. Make `step!` compute or store the injection after `update_forcing!` and before the stages.
- Resolution: _(author)_
